---
name: Gift experience scope
description: Product constraints for the personal romantic story experience.
---

Keep this app static and personal. Do not add a backend, authentication, or database, and do not invent shared memories or names. Keep editable personal content in one central place.

**Why:** The user wants a no-backend romantic gift whose personal details they will supply; invented history would misrepresent their relationship.

**How to apply:** When extending the experience, keep content client-side and generic until the user provides real personal details.
