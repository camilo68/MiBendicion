Keep this app static and personal. Do not add a backend, authentication, or database, and do not invent shared memories or names. Keep editable personal content in one central place.

Visual direction: Use a romantic, cosmic palette inspired by HUMBE's ARMAGEDÓN album—near-black, ember orange/red, molten gold, and warm ivory—with tasteful emojis throughout the experience.

Known relationship dates: they met on May 17 (the year was not specified), and officially began dating on September 14, 2026.

**Why:** The user wants a no-backend romantic gift whose personal details they will supply; invented history would misrepresent their relationship. The meeting date is intentionally yearless until they specify otherwise.

**How to apply:** Keep content client-side and generic until the user provides real personal details. Use only the known dates in the relationship story; do not add a year or circumstances for the first meeting. Keep future visual updates within the album-inspired palette and use emojis as accents, not clutter.
